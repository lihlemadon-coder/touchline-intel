Guide 01 / Coach choice

Start with the plan.

Choose a coach to support a playstyle you understand, not just for a headline rating. Coach effects and compatibility should be confirmed in the current game.

01

Pick a style you can execute

Consider how quickly you want to progress the ball, where you want to win it back, and how much space you are comfortable leaving behind.

02

Read the coach details

Review the in-game playstyle ratings and any listed boosts. Don't assume a coach improves player-pull odds; there is no verified basis for that claim.

03

Test before rebuilding

Try the coach in a few matches, then adjust one thing at a time. A familiar setup is often more useful than repeatedly chasing small rating changes.

Guide 02 / Squad building

Build a functioning XI.

Think in roles and relationships across the pitch. A squad is more than eleven high-rated cards.

01 / STRUCTURE

Choose a formation for your habits

Decide where you want passing options and defensive cover. Use the formation as a starting shape, then check how players occupy space in matches.

02 / ROLES

Balance the key jobs

Make sure the lineup can progress the ball, create chances, defend transitions, and protect the goal. Cover for aggressive runners with players who can hold position.

03 / DEPTH

Keep useful alternatives

Build a bench with different options for tired players or a match that needs a different approach. Check player position familiarity and condition in-game.

A simple review loop

Change, test, learn.

After a few matches, note where possession breaks down or opponents find space. Adjust one role, player, or tactical setting at a time so you can tell what helped.
