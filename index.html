Keep your game yours

Smart-pull principles

No hidden timing trick can turn an uncertain draw into a sure thing. Use a plan you can stick to.

01 / LIMIT

Choose a hard budget

Decide how many coins you are willing to use before opening a pack. Stop when you reach that limit.

02 / ODDS

Read the exact rate

Check whether the figure applies to your target player or to the pack as a whole. They are not interchangeable.

03 / COACH

Separate tactics from pulls

A coach can affect your squad's tactics and playstyle. There is no verified evidence that owning one improves player-pull odds.

Spend with a plan / eFootball Mobile

Buying Guide

A checklist for evaluating a pack and setting a coin budget, without treating a chance as a guarantee.

01 / SQUAD

Check the role first

Identify the position and role your squad actually needs. Compare a player with your current options before spending.

02 / ODDS

Read the pack details

Verify the player pool, per-draw target rate, pack cost, and any guarantees in the current in-game details.

03 / BUDGET

Set your stopping point

Choose an amount you are comfortable spending in advance. Don't chase a target by exceeding that limit.

04 / VERIFY

Ignore guaranteed-pull claims

Timing tricks, third-party generators, and rumors do not change the stated odds. Use official in-game information.

05 / COMPARE

Think about future options

Consider whether you would rather save coins for a different position or campaign. Offers and player availability change.

06 / DECIDE

Make it your call

Skip the pack if the possible outcome is not worth the cost to you. No player is essential to enjoy the game.
