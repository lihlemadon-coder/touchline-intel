How to read the tracker

Confirmed, not rumored.

Release timing and availability can differ by region, platform, and in-game campaign. Always check the game itself before making a decision.

01 / ANNOUNCED

Officially announced

Use this status only when an official Konami channel names the player or campaign. Record the announcement date and link to its original source.

02 / IN-GAME

Available in-game

Confirm the player, availability window, and pack details in the current game. An announcement alone does not confirm that an offer is still active.

03 / UNVERIFIED

Rumors stay out

Leaks, edited screenshots, and community speculation are not release confirmations. Don't plan a coin budget around unverified claims.

A quick safety check

Before you redeem.

Promotions can have eligibility rules, regional limits, and expiration dates. Follow the instructions attached to the original announcement.

01 / SOURCE

Find the original notice

Confirm the promotion on an official Konami channel or in-game News. A repost, screenshot, or comment is not proof by itself.

02 / DETAILS

Check terms and expiry

Read the eligible region, platform, redemption steps, and end date. A valid promotion may not apply to every account.

03 / ACCOUNT

Protect your login

Do not share passwords, verification codes, or account access to claim a reward. Avoid third-party generators and suspicious downloads.
