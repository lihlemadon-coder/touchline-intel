How to read the tracker

Confirmed, not rumored.

Release timing and availability can differ by region, platform, and in-game campaign. Always check the game itself before making a decision.

01 / ANNOUNCED

Officially announced

Use this status only when an official Konami channel names the player or campaign. Record the announcement date and link to its original source.

02 / IN-GAME

Available in-game

Confirm the player, availability window, and pack details in the current game. An announcement alone does not confirm that an offer is still active.

03 / UNVERIFIED

Rumors stay out

Leaks, edited screenshots, and community speculation are not release confirmations. Don't plan a coin budget around unverified claims.
